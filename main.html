About

Keegan 'KEEGZ' Anderson is a local DJ based out of Belleville, Ontario and the Quinte West Region.



DJing off and on since 2023 and professionally since 2026, KEEGZ has quickly made a name for himself in the local music scene with his energetic sets and lively crowds. KEEGZ style specializes in high energy house mixes as well as a wide variety of other genres. He has performed at various local venues and events and is looking to make your next night out unforgettable.



Book KEEGZ now for your next wedding, party, or event and watch the dance floor come alive!
