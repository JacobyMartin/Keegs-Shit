About

Keegan 'KEEGZ' Anderson is a Canadian DJ based out of Belleville, Ontario and the Quinte West Region.



DJing since 2023 and professionally since 2026, KEEGZ has quickly made a name for himself in the local music scene with his energetic sets and lively crowds. KEEGZ specalizes in a mix of high energy house, tech house, UKG, and dance music. Combining euphoric melodies, heavy baselines, and crowd favourite remixes, his sets are designed to keep energy high from start to finish. Whether it's a club set, party mix, or wedding performance, KEEGZ knows how to read the room and curate a vibe that keeps people dancing all night long.
